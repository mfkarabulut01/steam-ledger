# Steam Ledger / Steam Muhasebe Defteri

Chrome extension that shows your real Steam Community Market profit and loss after fees.
Not affiliated with Valve or Steam.

- Privacy policy / Gizlilik politikası: https://mfkarabulut01.github.io/steam-ledger/
- Support & feedback / Destek ve öneri: [Issues](https://github.com/mfkarabulut01/steam-ledger/issues)

`destek.json` is read by the extension (at most every 6 hours) for the optional, labeled sponsor card and the
donate/support links shown in the extension's own popup and dashboard. It contains data only, no code.
